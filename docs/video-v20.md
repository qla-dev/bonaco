# Approved Craft V20 film

The hero and Craft project use the final supplied landscape and portrait V20 compositions: Bonaco, Bonexis, Poslovni oglasi (03), natural-colour dance footage and the three-logo ending. Original duration and soundtrack are retained.

The original WebM exports are copied without changes. MP4 fallbacks are encoded directly from the corresponding ProRes masters at 7.2 Mbps to fit the hosting file limit, with the original audio packets copied unchanged. Both formats remain Full HD, 24 fps and 26.25 seconds (630 frames). Source/output hashes and full-decode validation are stored in the workspace outputs/v20-web/media-report.json. Older source media is retained as archive.
