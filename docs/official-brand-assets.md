# Official brand assets — 19 November 2026

The owner supplied the final Bonaco Media and Bonexis wordmarks and their compact B marks. The website uses those PNG exports, with transparent margins trimmed and the artwork resized for the web. No letterforms, dot positions or subtitles were redrawn.

Dark-background variants are SVG wrappers around the supplied artwork. A presentation filter makes neutral black lettering white while preserving the orange and teal brand elements. The original black variants remain available for light surfaces. The browser icon uses the supplied Bonaco B mark, without a background.

Brand colours sampled from the supplied files: Bonaco `#f5862a`, Bonexis `#009d9a`.
